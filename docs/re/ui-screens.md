## 1. Engine A layers

**[blue-verified]**, high for the layout (#62 B 1, C 1). DISPCNT `0x08817F10`: BG0 to BG3 and OBJ on, WIN0 and WIN1 on, char base 0, screen base +0x10000, all BGs 4bpp.

| Layer | BGCNT | Priority | Size | Screen base | Char base | Content |
|-------|-------|----------|------|-------------|-----------|---------|
| BG0 | `0x0000` | 0 | 256x256 | `0x06010000` | `0x06000000` | frame, text canvas, portrait (town frame), dungeon HUD (status line, minimap canvas, message-log frame and text, five touch buttons) |
| BG1 | `0x0101` | 1 | 256x256 | `0x06010800` | `0x06000000` | window fill: tile `0x27A` (blank) by default; tile `0x2DB` (blue) in cols 2 to 29, rows 17 to 21. Shown only through WIN0 (dungeon) |
| BG2 | `0x420A` | 2 | 512x256 | `0x06011000` | `0x06008000` | scene. Empty in the dungeon capture (map all 0) |
| BG3 | `0x440B` | 3 | 512x256 | `0x06012000` | `0x06008000` | dungeon floor, scrolled by the camera (see [BG3 floor scroll](#7-bg3-floor-scroll)) |

- OBJ: 1D mapping, 32-byte boundary. All dungeon sprites at priority 3; the minimap player dot at priority 0 (#62 C 1).
- Order at equal priority: OBJ over BG, lower BG number over higher (#62 C 1).
- Backdrop colour = palette entry 0.
- **Engine B** (top) has the same layout with its own copy of the font tiles (#62 B 1), but its UI was not covered (party menu is open, #62 C 5).
- The findings disagree on what BG2 and BG3 hold: B reads them as scene layers from three frames; C finds BG2 empty and BG3 as the floor in the dungeon. Both are consistent with the capture they cover.

## 4. Palettes

- BG palette 15 (both engines) = `system.sbin` `fontpal` palette 0. `fontpal` has 16 RGBX entries per palette (4 bytes each, 5-bit truncated after quantising). Entries are copied by `FUN_0201ae94` to RAM `0x021019D4` and written from palette index `0xF0` (#62 B 3, C 4). **[blue-verified]**, 16 of 16 colours.
- Palettes 1 and 2 to 6 of `fontpal` exist; in this ROM palette 1 is identical to palette 0. The selector (`FUN_02015fd8() == 1`) chooses between them. **[hypothesis]** for the meaning (not checked, B 3).
- BG palette 14 holds the portrait (`kao004` slot 0 palette, 16 of 16). **[blue-verified]** (#62 B 6).
- BG palette 14 in the dungeon is the `zmappat` RGBX palette (16 entries at file offset `0xcc10`). It matches the live palette for 15 of 16 colours (index 14 differs at run time). **[blue-verified]**, high (#62 C2). See [BG palette 14](#bg-palette-14). It is the same bank as the town portrait palette (B 6); the findings do not say whether both are live in one scene.
- OBJ palette 15 = `fontsppa` (16 colours) (#62 C 4). **[blue-verified]**.
- The colour indices used by the text (`#C` and the letter map) and their RGB values are in [text-engine.md](text-engine.md#colours).

## 6. WIN0 and the alpha log box

**[blue-verified]** for the table and the blend arithmetic; the table was read once (#62 C 4).

- `FUN_0201966c` (`02019738`) sets DMA 0 from `*(0x020F2138) + 4` into WIN0H (`0x04000040`). WIN0V = `0x00C0` (full height).
- Captured table at `0x020F2460`: every row is `0xFFFF` (empty) except rows 139 to 172, which are `0x13ED`, i.e. x 19 to 236.
- So the translucent log box is the rectangle x 19 to 236, y 139 to 172. The rounded look is the BG0 frame plus this rectangle.
- WINOUT = `0x003D` (BG1 not drawn outside the window). WININ = `0x3F3F`.
- **Blend:** BLDCNT = `0x1C42` (BG1 first target, alpha mode, second targets BG2, BG3 and OBJ), BLDALPHA = `0x0808`, so out = (BG1 x 8 + below x 8) / 16, where `below` is the floor or sprite pixel.
- Check: BG1 blue (32, 74, 106) over floor (164, 222, 0) gives (98, 148, 53); the capture has (96, 144, 48). **[blue-verified]** for the formula. The result is close, not exact: the capture is 2 to 5 units lower per channel, and the findings do not explain the gap.
- **Not verified:** whether the box resizes (not seen; the Rust renderer should use the constant rectangle). The 10 px in `dng1` (minimap dot corner, x 24 to 27, y 92 to 94) and 83 px in `b2` (around the leader and damage number, x 127 to 150, y 132 to 158) that do not match: sprite-animation or alpha related, not examined.

## 7. BG3 floor scroll

**[blue-verified]**, high (#62 C 2). This resolves the scroll point in the #61 notes. The floor is on BG3, not BG0.

- `FUN_02019268` (`FlushDisplayState`, called from the vblank commit at `02000c50`) copies the shadow arrays to the BG registers. Engine A shadow = `0x020F38D0` (8 signed 16-bit values: BG0 x, y through BG3 x, y). Engine B shadow = `0x020F38C0`. The copy writes the registers `0x04000010..1C` and `0x04001010..1C`; the findings give the literals as `DAT_020195c4` and `DAT_020195e0` without saying which is which.
- **Capture values** (shadow bytes): `dng1` BG3 = (-20, 460); `b2` BG3 = (4, 412). BG0 to BG2 are (0, 0) in both. These equal the camera s16 values at `+0x15F88` and `+0x15F8A` of #61 P4.
- The 512x256 map wraps: x mod 512, y mod 256 (460 becomes 204).
- Floor render test: sample BG3 at ((px + camx) mod 512, (py + camy) mod 256). **[blue-verified]**, high: the scratch compositor with this rule matches both frames (10 and 83 px differ, see [Not verified](#not-verified)).
- BG3 uses BG palettes 0 to 10 (the map's palette bits). Tile data at char base `0x8000`, tiles 1 to 983. The source is the #60 tileset (`bpc`/`bpl`); the identity of the palette and tile files was not re-matched here. **[hypothesis]** for the file ids.
- **Why it matters:** the BG scroll registers read back 0 (write-only), so a read of the registers gives no scroll. Read the shadow.

## Minimap and status line

**Module.** Addresses in this section are `ov_0001` (overlay 1, the dungeon) unless the text says otherwise. Plain addresses given without a module, such as `0x02105BD4` and `0x02078638`, are listed as the findings wrote them; the findings do not say which module they are in.

**Tags.** The minimap and status-line structure and the BG palette 14 match are **[blue-verified]**, high (decompile plus a capture match). The cell-choice conditions are **[blue-verified]** for the captured floor and **medium** in general. Meanings of the flags `+0x2e` and `+0x15FA8` are **[hypothesis]** or not traced (#62 C2).

### Minimap

- One 4x4-pixel cell per floor cell, drawn by `FUN_0213cd5c(x, y)`. The floor is 56 × 32 cells (x < 0x38, y < 0x20, as `FUN_02149a08` uses). The canvas is 224 × 128 px: BG0 cols 1 to 28, rows 1 to 16. A tile holds 2 × 2 cells.
- The canvas is a 4bpp tile bitmap in RAM at `*0x020E0788 + 0x15FC4` (row stride `0x380` = 28 tiles × 32 bytes). Changed tiles go to BG VRAM tiles `0x0B8` onward (destination `*0x021B4220`) through a queue in the dungeon struct: entries at `+0x19984` (12 bytes each, max `0x28`), count at `+0x19B64`, overflow flag at `+0x19B69`, and per-cell drawn flags at `+0x197C4` (`0x1C` per row).
- Callers repaint cells as they are discovered: 12 callers, including `FUN_0213c0f8`. The first write in the run was at frame 31, after the player moved, from `0213d2e8` through `0213d2ac`.
- Row and column: tile col = x / 2; tile row = (y − 2) / 2. When the entity-type byte `*(*0x021B4234 + 4)` is 6, rows start at y = 1, and row = (y − 1) / 2. Quadrant q = (x & 1) + 2 × ((y − 2 [+1]) & 1).
- Pattern store: `dungeon.sbin` `zmappat` (SIR0 at `0xcc50`). It holds 768 patterns of 64 bytes at file offsets `0x10` to `0xc010`, a table of 768 u32 pointers at `0xc010`, and an RGBX palette of 16 × 4 bytes at `0xcc10`. A pattern is 8 pairs of u32 `(AND mask, OR value)`, one pair per 8-pixel tile row (4bpp, 8 nibbles). The game does `tile_row = (tile_row & mask) | or`. The pattern for quadrant q touches only that quadrant's 4 × 4 pixels. Pattern index = `u*4 + q`, with u in 0 to 0xBF (192 cell patterns × 4 quadrants). A nibble with mask 15 and or 0 is kept; a nibble with mask 0 takes the or-value. The decoded catalogue (`zmap_catalog.txt`, all 192 patterns) is a scratch file, not in the repo.
- Choice of u, from the cell word `W = FUN_02149a08(x, y)`: `+0x00` terrain (u16), `+0x02` flags, `+0x0C` monster pointer, `+0x10` object pointer. The object's entity type comes from `*obj`: 1 monster, 2 trap, 3 item (see [turns.md](turns.md)).

| u | Pattern | When |
|---|---------|------|
| 0x01 | transparent (all 0 nibbles) | cell not discovered (`flags & 1 == 0`) and nothing else applies |
| 0x02 | red (idx 2) diamond | hostile monster visible: monster info `+6 != 0` |
| 0x0A | yellow (idx 6) diamond | a monster whose info `+0x40` is `'J'` or `'G'`, or status `+0x98` is 1 or 4, or an ally that is not the leader (`+6 == 0`, `+7 == 0`). The partner is yellow in the capture |
| 0x08 | white (idx 7) diamond | the leader (`+7 != 0`) when dungeon `+0x15FAC != 0`. Otherwise the leader cell shows terrain, and the position is stored at `0x021B3EB8` (x) and `+2` (y) for the OBJ dot |
| 0x03 | cyan (idx 5) diamond | an item on the floor (object entity type 3); also a trap-like object when `(trapVisible || flags & 2) && (terrain & 3) != 0` |
| 0x04 | red hourglass or X | a trap (type 2): `u = byte[0x021ABA30 + trapId]`, which is 4 for all ids except id 17, which gives 7 (cyan inner square) |
| 0x06 / 0x05 | cyan hollow square / yellow block | terrain flag `0x200` (stairs-like) with no object: 6 if `FUN_0206db90() == 0`, else 5. Meaning of the flag and predicate not traced (medium) |
| 0x0B | green (idx 4) diamond | any marked cell when dungeon `+0x15FA8` is set (monster or item "reveal" state). **[hypothesis]** |
| 0x20+m / 0x10+m | terrain: grey (idx 8) wall lines, or white (idx 7) lines with a hatch of idx 14 | floor cell (`terrain & 3 == 1`) discovered. `m` is the 4-neighbour wall mask: start at 0xF; clear bit 0 if (x, y+1) is floor, bit 2 if (x, y−1), bit 1 if (x+1, y), bit 3 if (x−1, y). A set bit draws a 1-px line on that side (bit 0 bottom, bit 1 right, bit 2 top, bit 3 left). Base 0x20 when `flags & 2 == 0`, 0x10 when set |
| +0x40 | 0x50+m (white lines, no hatch); 0x60+m (grey, the same as 0x20) | added when `FUN_0200c5dc() == 1` |
| +0x80 | 0x90+m, or 0x80.. (the same shapes with a solid idx-14 fill) | added when the entity-type byte `*(*0x021B4234 + 4) == 6` |

- The captured floor (dng1) draws white 1-px room walls with an empty interior, which is the 0x50+m family. Yellow diamond = partner. Red diamond = the wild Sunkern, one cell right of the leader. Cyan diamond = the floor item. No BG pixels at the leader cell: the leader is the blinking OBJ dot (tile 533, palette 15, at (24, 92)), which accounts for the 10 mismatched pixels in dng1. **[blue-verified]** for the capture, **medium** for the general mapping.
- Not verified: the exact flags in `W+2` (bit 0 discovered; bit 1 "room lit" is a guess), the predicates `FUN_0200c5dc`, `FUN_0206db90`, `FUN_0200c60c`, and the dungeon switches `+0x15FA2` to `+0x15FAC` (names only, from use).

### BG palette 14

**[blue-verified]**, high. Live BG palette 14 (engine A) equals the 16 RGBX entries at `zmappat` offset `0xcc10` (5-bit truncated) for 15 of 16 colours: index 0 is `0x1686`, 1 black, 2 red `0x001c`, 4 green `0x0380`, 5 cyan `0x7380`, 6 yellow `0x039c`, 7 white `0x7fff`, 8 grey `0x56b5`, 13 `0x3000`. Index 14 is `0x7000` in the file but `0x3524` in the capture, so it changes at run time. It is used only by the hatch and fill of the 0x1x and 0x9x terrain patterns, and that change is not traced. Engine B palette 14 is not checked.

### Status line (row 0)

**[blue-verified]**, high. `FUN_0213b764` (called from `FUN_0213bd94`) writes the 32-entry row-0 map shadow at `0x02105BD4` (module not stated; the findings give it as a plain address). The BG0 map is VRAM + `0x10000`, copied each frame by the generic copy at `0x02078638` (module not stated). Entries are `tile | 0xF000` (BG palette 15).

| BG0 column | Content |
|------------|---------|
| 0 | blank (`0x279` in the capture) |
| 1 | `B` (`0x2BE`) on basement floors; otherwise an `F` variant. The tiles `DAT 0xF2B8` and `DAT 0xF2BE` are chosen by the language switch `FUN_0202a040` (module not stated) |
| then | floor number from `FUN_0213b668` (1 or 2 digits), then `F` (`0x2B8`) |
| 5, 6 | `Lv` = `0x2B9`, `0x2BA` |
| 7 on | level digits (info `+9`; blank pad if below 10) |
| 9, 10 | `HP` = `0x2BB`, `0x2BC` |
| 11 on | current HP from `FUN_0213b4f8`, up to 3 digits. Hundreds from the table at `0x021AB918` (900 to 100, tiles `0x291` to `0x289`, alternate colour `0x2C8` to `0x2C0`); tens from `0x021AB960` (90 to 10); then the units digit; a blank pad tile `0x278` |
| 14 | `/` = `0x2BD` |
| 15 on | max HP; pad `0x278` |
| 18 to 29 | HP bar tiles `0x2CC + i` (12 entries) |

- Digits: digit d is `0x288 + d` (white outlined), or `0x2BF + d` (alternate colour, chosen when the flag at state `+0x2e` is 0). They are `system.sbin` `font` tiles (the font is 136 tiles at BG tile `0x278`, header word `0x88`): `0x288` to `0x291` are `'0'` to `'9'` (checked by an ASCII render), `0x2B8` is `F`, `0x2BB` is `H`, `0x2BC` is `P`, `0x2BD` is `/`, and `0x2BE` is `B`. Current HP above 999 shows a `??` glyph in the `0x2xx` range (`DAT 0x3E7` limit).
- HP and max HP: info `+0xe` and `+0x10` (u16). Level: info `+9` of the leader's info, reached through `gDungeon+0x15F80+0x10`, then entity, then `+0x70`.
- Low HP: if `0 < HP ≤ maxHP / 4` and the frame counter's bit 4 is set, BG palette 15 colours `0xFC` to `0xFF` are overwritten (a palette flash through `FUN_02011cb4` and `02011adc`, module not stated). `+0x36` and `+0x38` cache the last palette state.
- **HP bar** (`FUN_0213bc24(hp, maxHp, flag)`, **[blue-verified]**, high): builds 12 tiles of 32 bytes in RAM at `0x021B3D38` and copies them to BG VRAM `0x06005980` (tile `0x2CC`). The bar length in px is maxHP, capped at 96 px (12 tiles); above `0x5F` the fill is scaled as `hp × 96 / maxHp`. Each tile row: rows 0 and 7 transparent; rows 1 and 6 colour index 7 (white) over the bar length; rows 2 to 5 index 4 (green when flag ≠ 0, cyan index 5 when flag = 0) for the HP part, and index 2 (`0x2e1f`) for the missing part. Tiles shown = ceil(maxHP / 8) (state `+0x42`, max 12). In dng1, maxHP 34 gives 4 full tiles and 2 px.
- **Number fonts (OBJ, not the status line).** `hp5font` (516 bytes: a header and 16 tiles, digits 0 to 9, `-` and `+`), `levfont` and `etcfonta` are loaded by `FUN_021399b8` into globals `0x021B40B0/B4/B8` (the findings' shorthand). They are used for OBJ numbers (damage and heal sprites; tile 534 in the #62 B findings). `FUN_02139b3c(n, flag)` composes a number from `hp5font` tiles into a sprite tile buffer, 6 px per digit, and returns the width `(digits + 1) × 6`. **[blue-verified]** for the loader and composer; the exact OBJ slots are from findings B.
- The file at `0x021AB5F4` goes to OBJ VRAM `0x06403400` (tile 416). Its name was not read, because the string is not readable by a Ghidra memory read. `etcfont` goes to OBJ VRAM `0x06404000` (engine A) and `0x06604000` (engine B), tile 512.
